The location of your shop will play a significant role in determining its success. A prime location can lead to higher foot traffic and sales. Look for shops situated in high-visibility areas, near major roads, or within popular commercial districts.

2. Size and Layout
The size and layout of the shop are equally important. Assess your business needs—how much space do you require? Is the layout conducive to your operations? Be sure to visualize your business model within the space before making a decision.

Inspect the physical condition of the property. Consider whether you will need to invest in renovations or if the shop is move-in ready. Look for signs of wear and tear, and ensure that any necessary repairs are taken into account in your financial planning.
